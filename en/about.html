Hi, I’m  Alba.

I’ve spent more than five years working on other brands’ marketing. Over forty accounts: a sportswear brand, a law firm, a campsite… No two were alike, and that’s what I love most about every project.

Over the years I’ve learned that almost no company needs more ideas. What it needs is to know which of the ones it already has are working. So that’s where I start. I look at your numbers with the curiosity of someone opening a stranger’s drawer, find what brings you customers and what’s eating your budget, and explain it in plain words so we’re on the same page.

I don’t do it alone. We’re a team, each with her own obsession: ads, words, data, getting customers to come back. You talk to me, and together we make things happen.
